First-Auto EV Board

What buying a player's base 1st Bowman Chrome auto is worth this November, held until he debuts (sold in days 0–3 of the call-up) or until next October. Production v3.5 probabilities, SportsCardsPro sold prices, eBay asks pulled 22 Oct 2026.

How EV is built

E[exit] = P(debut '27) × Mdebut + (1 − P) × Mmiss

The multiples come from 3,172 November positions (2022–25) per price band; the debut pop from ~200 players' sale-level debut windows. The miss path is higher for likely new Top-100 entrants. Costs: 13.25% + $0.40 to sell, 7% tax + $1.50 shipping to buy.

Max pay is the price at which EV is zero. Buy a listing only under it.

What held up out of sample

  • EV above +10% realized +17% raw and +18% PSA 10 (leave-one-year-out); above +20% realized +26% raw.
  • EV between 0 and +10% lost money on raw cards.
  • At the fair price only the near-certain call-ups clear: P ≈ 0.65–0.8 earns +15–22% (all-years market), +30–40% if 2027 trades like 2024–25.
  • The market year swings everything: non-debuters returned 0.74× in 2022 and 1.11× in 2025.

Which debuts pop more

  • Days 0–3 pop measured on ~200 players' base autos and every priced parallel: 2.5× under $10, about 1.5× above $15.
  • Hype already in the price shrinks it: ever-Top-100 names popped 1.38× vs 1.78×, mostly through price. September call-ups pop less.
  • Team market and early-ETA effects did not replicate on sale-level data. The pop index is shown, not used.
  • Long run: a settled star's raw auto averages $44, an established player's $9. Holding past the pop is a star lottery.
